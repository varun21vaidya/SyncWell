# Privacy Policy for SyncWell

**Effective Date:** June 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** June 2, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

SyncWell ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a personal fitness and wellness tracking application developed for iOS and Android platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you use our mobile application, websites, and related services.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices. Please read this policy carefully.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration**
- Full name
- Email address
- Password (encrypted)
- Date of birth (optional)
- Profile information (bio, photo)

**App Usage**
- Fitness routines you create
- Workout logs and exercise data
- Daily routine completions
- Settings preferences

### 2.2 Health & Activity Data

**Health Connect Integration (Android)**

SyncWell integrates with Android Health Connect to retrieve health data with your explicit permission. We only access what you authorize:

- **Steps per day** - Used for step tracking dashboard
- **Heart rate** (if enabled) - Used for health insights
- **Sleep data** (if enabled) - Used for wellness analytics
- **Calories burned** (if enabled) - Used for fitness tracking

**Important**: 
- You control Health Connect permissions in your Android Settings
- You can revoke access at any time
- No health data is sent to our servers by default (stored locally on device)
- Data is only synced to our backend if you enable cloud backup

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

**Device Information**
- Device model and OS version
- App version
- Crash reports and error logs
- Unique device identifier (for analytics only)

**Usage Analytics**
- Features you use
- Time spent in app
- Navigation patterns
- Feature adoption metrics

**Sentry Error Tracking**
- Stack traces from crashes
- Error contexts
- Breadcrumbs (user action history)
- Device state at time of error

## 4. Data Storage & Location

### 4.1 Local Storage
- All routine data, workout logs, and daily tracking stored locally on device
- Encrypted using device's secure storage
- Only synced to cloud if you enable backup

### 4.2 Cloud Storage (Firebase Firestore)
- Backup data stored in Google Cloud (US region)
- Uses Firebase security rules and encryption
- Accessible only by authenticated users

### 4.3 Health Data
- Health Connect data never leaves your device by default
- Only sent to our servers if you explicitly enable sync
- Immediately deleted from our servers per your preferences

## 6. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

### 6.1 Access & Portability
- Request a copy of your data at any time
- Export your fitness data in standard format (JSON)
- Access detailed health metrics

### 6.2 Correction & Updates
- Edit your profile information
- Update account settings
- Modify data retention preferences

### 6.3 Deletion
- Delete specific workout sessions
- Clear daily tracking history
- Request full account deletion (data deleted within 30 days)

### 6.4 Health Data Control
- Revoke Health Connect permissions in Android Settings
- Stop syncing health data to our servers
- Local health data remains on device

### 6.5 Notifications
- Disable routine reminders
- Disable supplement/water reminders
- Configure notification frequency

### 6.6 Analytics
- Opt-out of usage analytics (settings)
- Disable Sentry error tracking (settings)
- Remain pseudonymous in analytics

## 7. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Policy |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Account data | Until deletion | Retained until you delete account |
| Routine/Workout logs | 2 years | Archived after 2 years of inactivity |
| Daily tracking | 1 year | Deleted after 1 year |
| Health data | Until revoked | Deleted when Health Connect access revoked |
| Error logs | 30 days | Automatically purged after 30 days |
| Analytics | 90 days | Aggregated, not identifiable after 90 days |
| Backups | User-controlled | Deleted when user disables backup |

## 8. Security

### 8.1 Data Protection
- All data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Sensitive data encrypted at rest
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt
- Health data never transmitted without encryption

### 8.2 Security Practices
- Regular security audits
- Error tracking with Sentry for security issues
- Secure authentication via Firebase
- Rate limiting on API endpoints
- HTTPS enforced for all connections

### 8.3 Your Responsibilities
- Keep your password secure
- Use strong passwords
- Don't share account access
- Monitor account activity
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## 9. Children's Privacy
