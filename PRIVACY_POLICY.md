# Privacy Policy for SyncWell

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026
**Contact:** varunvaidyadev@gmail.com

## What SyncWell is
SyncWell is an Android app for tracking daily routines, workouts, focus sessions, tasks, water, supplements, steps and body weight.

## Data stays on your device
Everything you enter is stored locally in a database on your phone. We run no servers, and we do not receive, sell or share your data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Data the app stores
Routines and checklist history, workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights), focus/tracker sessions, to-dos, water, supplement and step logs, body weight, and your settings and onboarding answers (wake/sleep time, workout days, water goal, weight goal).

## Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup
Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google and use backup:
- The app reads your Google account email and name to identify the account.
- It requests only the Google Drive scope `drive.appdata`. This gives SyncWell access to its own hidden app-data folder in your Drive. It cannot see, list or read any of your other Drive files.
- A single backup file containing the data listed above is saved to that folder in **your** Google account. A scheduled backup may run daily while backup is enabled.
- The backup is stored by Google under your account and is subject to Google's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time from Google Drive (Settings > Manage apps > SyncWell > Delete hidden app data) or revoke access in your Google Account (Security > Third-party access).

## Permissions
- **Internet:** only to talk to Google for sign-in and Drive backup.
- **Notifications:** reminders and the focus timer notification.
- **Foreground service:** keeps the focus timer running while the app is in the background.

## Children
SyncWell is not directed to children under 13.

## Your choices
Use the app without signing in. Delete backups from Google Drive. Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove all local data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
