Perfect Workouts — Copiable Format
Push Day — Chest + Shoulders + Triceps
Rest: 2–3 min after compounds | 60–90 sec after isolation
Exercise	Sets × Reps (Weight)	Notes
Pushups	1 set × 100	Warmup only, not to failure. Full ROM: chest near floor, slow descent 2 sec down. Stop at 15 regardless. Do not pre-exhaust chest before bench.
Barbell Flat Bench Press	15-15-15 kg (one side) × 15-14-12	Do not bend wrists — palm slightly angled inward. Lower bar to mid-chest, 2 sec down. Shoulder blades pinched back and down into bench. Feet flat on floor. Increase weight only when all 4 sets hit 8 reps cleanly. This is your anchor compound lift.
Dumbbell Overhead Shoulder Press	15-15-15 kg (each hand) × 15-15-15	Sit on 90° upright bench. Start at shoulder height, elbows at 90°. Press straight up — do not touch DBs at top, keep tension. Do not arch lower back. Wrists straight — not bent back. Control 2 sec on the way down. Core braced throughout.
Incline Dumbbell Press	15-15-15 kg (each hand) × 15-15-12	Bench angle 30–45° only — higher becomes shoulder press. Elbows at 75°, not fully flared out. Lower to upper chest, full stretch at bottom. Hard squeeze at top. Wrists neutral. This replaces assisted incline machine — better ROM and balance.
Lateral Raises	8-8-8 kg (each hand) × 15-15-12	Slight 10–15° forward lean from waist — do not stand fully upright. Lead with elbows, not wrists. Raise to shoulder height only — no higher or traps take over. Pinky slightly higher than thumb at top. 3 sec slow lowering — the eccentric builds side delts. No momentum — if swinging, weight is too heavy.
Lateral Raises Cable	10-10-10 kg (each hand) × 10	Put D handle below 18; can increase.
Pec Fly Machine	50-50-50 kg × 15-15-10	Slight permanent bend in elbows throughout — never straight arms. Squeeze hard at full closure, hold 1 sec. Control the return — 3 sec opening. Sit tall, chest up, back flat against pad. Increased from 45 kg — increase weight only when all 3×12 done cleanly.
Tricep Pushdown — Bar (Week A) / Rope (Week B)	Bar: 32.5-32.5-32.5 kg × 12-12-10 / Rope: 27.5-27.5-27.5 kg × 12-12-10	Upper arms fixed to sides — elbows must not drift forward or move at all. Do not focus on pulling bar down — focus on straightening the elbow fully. Full lockout at bottom, hold 1 sec squeeze. Slow return 2 sec. Alternate bar and rope each push session.
Cable Crossover (Alternate each session)	15-20-20 kg × 20-20-15	Week A: Upper cable (high to low). Week B: Lower cable (low to high). Lean forward slightly from waist. One hand at a time — lean from waist not shoulder. Do not push from shoulder — push from side chest like wings. Hands meet at chest center, hold squeeze 1 sec. Elbows always soft — never lock straight or it becomes a press.
Tricep Overhead Extension (both hands)	20-20-20 kg × 10-10-10	Elbows pointing straight up — do not let them flare sideways. Lower dumbbell behind head until full long-head stretch. Press up without moving upper arms at all. This is the only exercise hitting tricep long head — never skip this. Increased from 15 kg.
Dumbbell Pullover (Finisher — only if energy remains)	20-20-20 kg × 10-10-10	Lie across bench, hips low. Arms slightly bent throughout. Arc the DB behind head until full chest/serratus stretch. Bring back to chest level — stop when upper arms reach parallel, do not overstretch. 2 sets max. Skip if session already at 65+ mins.

Pull Day — Back + Biceps + Rear Delts + Forearms
Rest: 2–3 min after compounds | 60–90 sec after isolation
Exercise	Sets × Reps (Weight)	Notes
Pull-ups	BW × 15-10	Anchor compound. Full dead hang at bottom — do not half rep. Pull until chin clears bar. Slow 2 sec lowering — this is where lats grow. Do not swing or kip. When all 3 sets hit 15-15-15 cleanly, add a 2.5 kg plate belt. Currently at 24-13 over 2 sets — restructuring to 3 quality sets.
Deadlift	50-50-60 kg × 15-15-15	Do not skip. Bar over mid-foot. Hips hinge back — not squat down. Back flat, chest up, proud chest. Drive floor away with legs, do not pull with back. Lock hips fully at top. Controlled descent. Start at 60 kg and add 5 kg only when form is perfect across all 5 reps. Record this every session.
Lat Pulldown Front	45-45-45 kg × 15-15-12	Pull bar to upper chest — not behind neck ever. Lean back 10–15° only. Lead with elbows driving down and back — not with hands. Squeeze shoulder blades together at bottom. Move shoulders inward as you pull. Slow 2 sec return — do not let weight snap up.
Dumbbell Row	20-20-20 kg × 15-15-15	Use a bench or slightly elevated surface for support. Look sideways — not down. Keep back flat — parallel to floor. Pull DB to hip, not shoulder. Elbow should not go past body at top. Pretend you are picking something up from the floor — same hip-hinge energy.
Seated Cable Row	90-90-90 kg × 15-15-15	Sit upright — do not lean back behind 90°. Pull handle to belly button. Squeeze shoulder blades hard at peak contraction. Slow return — let back fully stretch forward. Do not shrug shoulders up. Fixed from inconsistent 90 kg→45 kg — standardized at working weight.
Face Pull	25-25-25 kg × 15-15-15	Cable should be set at face height — not horizontal, angle must be pulling toward your face vertically. Pull rope to forehead/ears level, both hands. Elbows flare out wide at peak. Focus on rear delts and external rotation — hold 1 sec squeeze. Never skip this — protects shoulder joint and rotator cuff long term.
Rear Delt Dumbbell Raise	2.5-2.5-2.5 kg (each) × 10-10	Slight forward lean from waist. Arms slightly bent, fixed throughout. Lead with elbows driving backward and outward — squeeze rear delts at top. Do not use momentum. Slow 3 sec lowering. Complements face pull — together these keep shoulders balanced and injury-free.
DB Bicep Curl (supinated, alternating)	12.5-12.5-12.5 kg (each) × 12-12-10	Supinated grip — palm faces up at top. Alternate hands. Keep upper arm completely still — elbow as fixed pivot. Do not let elbow drift forward. Slow 2 sec lowering. Squeeze at top. Do not swing torso. Can sit on bench for better stability.
Hammer Curl (neutral grip, alternating)	12.5-12.5-12.5 kg (each) × 12-12-10	Palms face each other throughout — thumb on top. Keep biceps constant — do not move elbow or shoulder. Slow and controlled. Builds brachialis and brachioradialis — different from supinated curl. Do it slowly in form as noted. This is different enough from DB curl above to keep both.
Preacher Curl DB (each hand)	12.5-12.5-12.5 kg × 12-12-12	Elbow fully planted on pad — must not lift off. Lower DB until arm nearly straight — full stretch at bottom is key. Slow 3 sec lowering. Do not heave or swing at bottom. Focus on form over reps as noted. Best exercise for bicep peak.
Forearm Wrist Curl — Rod	Rod × 15-15	Use barbell rod. Both hands pronated (palms down). Curl wrists up fully, lower completely. 2 sets only as finisher. Covers wrist extensors and flexors together.

Legs + Abs Day — Quads + Hamstrings + Glutes + Calves + Core
Rest: 2–3 min after compounds | 60–90 sec after isolation | No rest between abs
Exercise	Sets × Reps (Weight)	Notes
Free Squats (Warmup)	BW × 20-20	Warmup only — comes before weighted squats, not after. Full depth, knees tracking over toes, chest up. Do not go to failure. 2 sets max.
Weighted Barbell Squat	30-30-30 kg (one side) × 15-15-15	Use belt. Bar on upper traps, not neck. Feet shoulder-width, toes 15–30° outward. Knees track over pinky toe — do not cave inward. Break parallel — thighs at least parallel to floor. Chest tall throughout. Drive through heels to stand. This is your primary quad + glute compound — never skip.
Leg Press	78-78-78 kg × 15-15-15	Feet mid-platform, shoulder width. Do not push until legs become fully straight — slight bend always remains at top. When returning, do not bring knees past 90° (7 holes visible from back as noted). Adjust seat distance so you can release and push without butt lifting off.
Romanian Deadlift (RDL)	40-40-40 kg × 10-10-10	New — do not skip. This is your missing hamstring compound. Bar stays close to legs throughout. Hinge from hips — not squat down. Lower bar until hamstring stretch is felt (mid-shin level). Back flat and braced throughout. Do not round lower back. Drive hips forward to return. This is different from a deadlift — it is a hinge with fixed knees.
Leg Extension	60-60-60 kg × 15-12-12	Adjust pad so it sits at lower shin — not on foot. Full extension at top — lock out and hold 1 sec squeeze. Slow 3 sec lowering — this eccentric builds quad sweep. Do not swing. Sit fully back in seat.
Leg Curl	60-60 kg × 15-15	Adjust the side bar at legs according to height — only one hole visible at end as noted. Curl until hamstring fully contracted. Slow 3 sec lowering — do not let weight snap back. Do not lift hips off pad. Standardized from 50-50-40 — pick one working weight.
Leg Lunges	15-15-15 kg (each hand) × 12-12-12 (each leg)	Step forward far enough so front knee stays behind toes at bottom. Back knee touches or hovers near floor. Push through front heel to return. Keep torso upright — do not lean forward. Alternating legs each rep. Functional glute + quad finisher.
Calf Raises	25-25-25 kg × 20-20-20	Full ROM — heels below platform level at bottom (full stretch), all the way up on toes at top (peak squeeze). Pause 1 sec at top. Slow 3 sec lowering. Calves are slow-twitch — they need higher reps and full ROM to grow. Increased from 20 kg.
ABS FINISHER
No rest between exercises, 60 sec rest between rounds
Exercise	Sets × Reps	Notes
Hanging Leg Raises	3 × 15-15-12	Full dead hang. Raise legs to parallel or above. Do not swing hips — core lifts legs, not momentum. Slow 2 sec lowering. If 15 reps easy, add ankle weight. Best lower ab exercise.
Cable Crunches	3 × 15-15-15 @ 50 kg	Kneel facing cable. Pull rope to sides of head — do not pull with arms. Crunch by bringing elbows toward knees — spinal flexion, not hip flexion. Hold squeeze 1 sec at bottom. Slow return. This is your only weighted ab exercise.
Decline Crunches	3 × 20-20-20	Arms crossed on chest or hands behind head — do not pull neck. Curl upper spine up, not full sit-up. Hold 1 sec at top. Slow lowering 2 sec.
Russian Twists	3 × 30-30-30	Feet elevated off floor for harder version. Twist to touch floor each side — one twist each side = 1 rep. Can hold a weight plate for progressive overload. Slow and controlled — feel obliques contracting.
Plank	3 × 60-75-60 sec	Forearms or hands. Hips in line — do not sag or pike. Glutes and core braced, not just holding breath. Breathe normally. Progress by adding time each week. Side plank folded in — do 30 sec each side on the last plank set.

--------------------
Rules
• 5 min warmup walk + 1–2 light sets first exercise.
• Rest: 2–3 min compounds, 60–90 sec isolation.
• Progress: all top reps hit → +2.5 kg next time.
• Stop at 55–70 min. Form breaks → end session.
• Track: Date | Exercise | Weight | Reps | Notes.
--------------------
Cardio
• Daily: 3 km office walk.
• Weekend Sat: 20 min HIIT OR 40 min badminton/running.
• Hometown: Bodyweight 3 days/week + walks.
--------------------
Critical Form
• Bench: Wrists straight, scapulae retracted.
• Deadlift: Flat back, bar over mid-foot.
• Squat: Knees track toes, thighs parallel.
• Pull-up: Dead hang, no kipping.
• Face Pull: Rope to forehead, rear delts only.
• Tricep Pushdown: Elbows pinned to ribs.
• Lateral Raise: Elbows lead, shoulder height max.
• RDL: Hip hinge, bar close to legs.
• Abs: No neck pull, slow negatives.
--------------------
Target
• Month 1: 70 kg → 68.5 kg (upper abs outline)
• Month 2: 68.5 → 67 kg (4-pack visible)
• Month 3: 67 → 65.5 kg (six-pack clear)
• Month 4+: Maintain + refine
--------------------
HIIT
• Minutes 0–2: Warmup walk (4–5 kmph)
• Minutes 2–3: Sprint 1 (9–12 kmph or 80–90% effort)
• Minutes 3–4: Walk recovery (3–4 kmph)
• Minutes 4–5: Sprint 2 (same max effort)
• Minutes 5–6: Walk recovery
• Continue for 8 total sprints.
• Minutes 18–20: Cool-down walk
